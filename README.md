# 🚀 Instant Context
**Know the truth about any webpage in seconds.**

### 🌟 Impact: Why use this?
Webpages can be old and wrong. If a news story breaks today, an "About" page might not update for months. 
- **The Problem**: You are reading an article, but you aren't sure if the information is still true.
- **The Result**: With one click, this tool checks live news for you. It catches "hidden" changes—like a new CEO being named—that the website hasn't listed yet. It saves you from being tricked by outdated information.

---

### ℹ️ Information: What is it?
Instant Context is a helper for your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Brave). 
- **Live Chat**: Ask any question about the page you are reading.
- **Deep Search**: It doesn't just read the page; it searches the real-time internet to double-check the facts.
- **Smart Summary**: It can turn a long, boring article into 10 easy-to-read bullet points.

---

### 🛠️ Action: How to run it (Step-by-Step)
You don't need to be a computer expert to use this. Just follow these 4 steps:

**Step 1: Download the folder**
- Download this project folder to your computer's Desktop.

**Step 2: Open your Extensions page**
- Open your Google Chrome browser.
- Type `chrome://extensions` in the top bar and press **Enter**.
- In the top-right corner, turn on the switch that says **"Developer mode"**.

**Step 3: Load the tool**
- Click the button on the left that says **"Load unpacked"**.
- Find the project folder on your Desktop and click **"Select Folder"**.
- You will now see the "Instant Context" icon in your browser!

**Step 4: Add your "Magic Keys"**
- Click the extension icon in your browser.
- Click the **Settings (⚙)** gear icon.
- Paste your Gemini and Serper API keys into the boxes and click **Save**. (You can get these keys for free from Google AI Studio and Serper.dev).

---

### 🛡️ Responsibility: How we stay safe
- **Privacy First**: We never see your API keys. They are stored only on your computer inside your browser.
- **No Hidden Reading**: The tool only reads the page when you specifically ask it to.
- **Simple Tech**: No heavy installation or complex coding required—just clean, fast, and private fact-checking.
